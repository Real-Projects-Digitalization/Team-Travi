# Travi — Travel Smarter 🌍✈️  
**Swipe. Match. Travel. Together.**

---

## The Problem

Group travel can be chaotic—messy WhatsApp threads, countless app-switching, indecisive group chats, and lost booking confirmations. For Gen Z especially, planning should be fast, fun, and frictionless. Yet, today's solutions are fragmented, uninspired, and far from playful.

---

## The Solution: Travi

**Travi** is your all-in-one group travel app. We turn stressful planning into smart, seamless, and social travel experiences.

### 🚀 Features

- **AI-Powered Trip Planning**: Tailor-made recommendations based on interests, budget, and group dynamics.
- **Swipe & Match Travel Discovery**: Playfully discover destinations as a group.
- **Real-Time Coordination**: Schedule, vote, and plan together in one place—no more endless chats.
- **Collaborative Budgeting**: Track expenses and bookings transparently across the group.
- **Integrated Platform**: No switching between apps—we combine planning, booking, communication, and cost management.

---

## Why Travi?

- **Gen Z Focused**: Fast, intuitive, mobile-first experience inspired by TikTok-style usability.
- **Gamified UX**: Swipe mechanics and fun UI that boosts engagement and decision-making.
- **Built for Teams**: Unlike individual-focused tools, Travi is designed for social, shared travel.
- **Privacy-First & Scalable**: Committed to user data protection and long-term growth potential.
