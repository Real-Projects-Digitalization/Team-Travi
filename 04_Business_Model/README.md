## 📁 04_Business_Model

This folder includes all documents related to the value creation logic and strategic planning of the Travi project. It outlines how the app delivers value, differentiates itself from competitors, and targets key customer segments.

### Files:

- **The Business Model Canvas.pdf**  
  A one-page overview of the business model, showing key partners, activities, value propositions, customer segments, and revenue streams.

- **Explanation of the Business Model Canvas.pdf**  
  A detailed guide explaining the components of the Business Model Canvas and how they apply to Travi.

- **Value Proposition Canvas.pdf**  
  A focused breakdown of customer jobs, pains, and gains, and how Travi's features are designed to meet them.

- **Competition AI planner.xlsx**  
  An AI-based planning tool used to analyze and compare competitive offerings in the trip planning space.

- **Business Model.xlsx**  
  Preliminary business planning spreadsheet containing key figures and financial planning logic. This includes early estimates for pricing, user growth, revenue potential, and cost structures.
